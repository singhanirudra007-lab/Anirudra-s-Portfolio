Challenge

The original Yale School of Art website had strong artistic character but lacked usability and structural clarity. Users faced difficulty navigating information due to visual clutter, inconsistent layouts, weak hierarchy, and an overwhelming information architecture. The challenge was to preserve the creative essence of the website while making it intuitive, accessible, and user-centered.

challenge visual — waiting for upload

Process

The redesign began with a detailed UX audit of the existing Yale School of Art website to understand its usability challenges and overall user experience. I evaluated the website through heuristic analysis, focusing on navigation, visual hierarchy, content organization, readability, and consistency. The audit helped identify key pain points that were affecting how easily users could find and understand information. Based on these findings, I restructured the information architecture and simplified the navigation system. I then developed cleaner layouts with improved typography, spacing, content grouping, and visual hierarchy. The goal was to create a more intuitive and accessible experience while still retaining the distinctive artistic character of Yale School of Art.

process visual — waiting for upload

Outcome

The redesigned website transforms the original experience into a cleaner, more structured, and visually balanced interface. Information is organized in a way that makes important content easier to discover and understand. Navigation was simplified to help users move between sections with less effort and confusion. Improved typography, spacing, and content hierarchy create a more readable and consistent experience across the website. The redesign also introduces a stronger visual system that brings together the academic and artistic nature of the institution. Overall, the project demonstrates how a thoughtful UX approach can improve usability without compromising the original character of a brand or institution.
